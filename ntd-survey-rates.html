What every survey includes

  • Nameplate data: make, model, serial, age, refrigerant, tonnage
  • Operational check in cooling and heating modes
  • Refrigerant readings: suction/discharge pressure, superheat, subcooling
  • Amp draw on compressors, condenser fans, blower motor
  • Electrical: contactors, capacitors, wiring, disconnect
  • Coils, drain pan, condensate line, filters, belts, bearings
  • Cabinet, panels, curb, and visible duct connections
  • Thermostat / control operation and any active fault codes
  • Photos of every deficiency
